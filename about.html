What is BookTree?

Short answer: BookTree is a book recommendation site made in 2026, aimed at (but not exclusively for) new readers, of all ages, genders and interests

Why it was created

The creator of BookTree, "name", loves reading, and wants other people to also enjoy reading. The problem is that people that want to read, often don't know where to start. Or they get too pressured about reading, causing them to give up. That's why BookTree's number one policy is Availability, Not Force. BookTree wants non-readers to want to start reading, and therefore choose to come here. Not be forced to. And we also want to give them recommendations that fit their own interests and tastes.

How BookTree works

In the menu at the top, you can let your mouse hover above the button "Start Reading". A submenu will appear. On the submenu, you can choose between different genres and categories, or click "Unsure" if you don't know or would just like to look around. Right now, the site isn't finished, but "Unsure" will contain the longest list of books on the site.
